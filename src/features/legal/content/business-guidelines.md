**Winnbell - Business Participation Guidelines**

**1\. Purpose**

These Guidelines provide practical instructions for Participating Businesses on how to promote and operate within Winnbell Campaigns. They form part of the [Participating Business Agreement](/business-agreement) and must be followed as a condition of participation. Capitalized terms in this agreement shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the Agreement.

Winnbell operates all Campaigns as the sole operator and administrator. Participating Businesses act only as marketing partners that enable customer access to participation opportunities.

**2\. Core Principles**

Participating Businesses must follow these core principles:

- **2.1** Winnbell is fully responsible for Campaign operation, including entries and winner selection
- **2.2** Entries are created only through the Winnbell platform
- **2.3** No purchase is required to participate
- **2.4** Customers must not be misled about how participation works

**3\. Staff Behavior and Day-to-Day Conduct**

**3.1** Participating Businesses must ensure that all staff:

- (a) Explain the Campaign in a simple and accurate way.
- (b) Do not improvise or create their own explanations of how the Campaign works.
- (c) Do not make promises about winning or chances of winning.
- (d) Do not suggest that a purchase is required to participate.
- (e) Treat the Campaign as a Winnbell promotion, not a promotion run by the business.
- (f) If unsure, staff should not improvise or create their own explanations and should direct customers to the Winnbell platform.

**3.2** Transactions must be real, normal customer transactions. Staff must not create or assist with fake or unusual transactions to help generate participation.

**4\. Prohibited Conduct**

**4.1** Participating Businesses must not:

- (a) present any Campaign as their own promotion;
- (b) state or imply that customers must buy something to participate;
- (c) suggest that buying more increases the chance of winning beyond the Campaign rules;
- (d) create, issue or attempt to control entries;
- (e) help customers submit false or misleading information;
- (f) misrepresent how a Campaign works in any way;
- (g) condition, restrict or present any Winnbell additional participation mechanism (including QR codes or links) as dependent on a purchase, transaction, or customer action; or
- (h) represent additional participation mechanisms, including Bonus Entries (as defined in the Official Rules), as being issued, controlled or provided by the business.

**5\. Customer Communication Guidelines**

**5.1** All customer-facing communication must be clear, accurate and consistent with Campaign rules. Each Campaign must always be presented as part of Winnbell, not as a promotion run by the business. Marketing must not create the impression that making a purchase is required to participate.

Example:

- ❌ "Buy this to get a chance to win"
- ✅ "Customers can access the Winnbell Campaign through their transaction"

**5.2** Businesses must not hide or ignore the fact that participation is available without purchase.

**6\. Marketing Materials and POS Promotion**

Winnbell provides pre-approved marketing materials (including posters and POS content) through the platform. Use of these materials is strongly recommended, as they are designed to ensure compliant and consistent communication.

Businesses may use their own marketing materials, provided such materials comply with these Guidelines and applicable laws.

Any custom campaign-related materials must include the following disclosure statement exactly as written, without modification:

"This business participates in campaigns operated by Winnbell. Alternative method of entry available. No purchase necessary to enter or win. Each entry has equal odds of winning, regardless of method of entry. 18+. Void where prohibited. Participation opportunities may vary by business and campaign availability. Official Rules at Winnbell.com"

The disclosure statement must be displayed in a clear and reasonably readable manner.

These elements ensure customers understand that the Campaign is operated by Winnbell and that free participation is available.

**7\. Cooperation**

Participating Businesses may be asked to assist with basic verification (for example, confirming a transaction). Businesses are expected to cooperate reasonably when requested.

**8\. Enforcement**

If a Participating Business does not follow these Guidelines, Winnbell may require changes to marketing or staff behavior, suspend or limit participation, or remove the business from a Campaign.
