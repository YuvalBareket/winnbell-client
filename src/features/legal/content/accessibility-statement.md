## Our Commitment

Winnbell is committed to ensuring that its website and web application are accessible to people with disabilities. We continually work to improve the accessibility of our platform so that everyone can participate fully.

## Conformance Status

We target conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities. Conformance with these guidelines helps make the web more user-friendly for everyone.

## Measures We Have Taken

To support accessibility, Winnbell takes the following measures:

- **Semantic markup** - We use proper HTML landmarks, headings, and ARIA roles so that screen readers can navigate content efficiently.
- **Keyboard operability** - All interactive controls are reachable and operable by keyboard without requiring a mouse.
- **Color contrast** - We maintain a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text and 3:1 for large text and graphical elements.
- **Screen reader labels** - Icon-only buttons and form controls carry accessible names via aria-label or associated label elements.
- **Text resize and zoom** - The platform supports browser zoom and text resizing; pinch-zoom is never blocked.
- **Motion and animation** - Key animations respect the operating system's reduced-motion preference.
- **Alternative Method of Entry** - A free Alternative Method of Entry to all draws is available for any person. Contact us at support@winnbell.com to request assistance.

## Known Limitations

While we work hard to achieve and maintain WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, some areas of our platform may have limitations we have not yet addressed. We welcome reports of any barriers you encounter.

## Feedback and Contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of the Winnbell platform, or if you need information in an alternative format, please contact us:

**Email:** support@winnbell.com

We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within 5 business days. We will work with you to provide the information or functionality you need through an accessible alternative.

## Technical Approach

We use automated accessibility testing as part of our development process and continue to expand our testing with keyboard navigation and assistive technologies.
