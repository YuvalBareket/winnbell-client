**Winnbell Cancellation & Refund Policy**

**(Participating Businesses only)**

**1. Overview**

This Cancellation & Refund Policy (the "Policy") explains how cancellation of Campaign participation and Subscriptions is handled, including any applicable refunds or financial adjustments.

This Policy forms part of the Winnbell Participating Business Agreement (the "Agreement") and applies to all Participating Businesses. Capitalized terms in this agreement shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the Agreement.

**2. Cancellation Before First Payment**

A Participating Business that has enrolled in a Subscription but has not yet been charged may cancel its enrollment at any time before the applicable Auto Payment Date. If enrollment is cancelled before the Auto Payment Date, no subscription fee will be charged and the Participating Business will not be enrolled in the upcoming Campaign unless it enrolls again.

**3. Subscription Cancellation by the Participating Business**

**3.1 Cancellation of Subscription**

A Participating Business may cancel its Subscription at any time through the Platform or by any other method made available by the Provider.

Cancellation applies only to future automatic renewals and does not affect any Campaign or Subscription period for which payment has already been successfully processed.

**3.2 No Refund After Payment**

Once a subscription fee has been successfully processed, it becomes non-refundable. Cancellation of a Subscription does not entitle the Participating Business to any refund for the current Campaign, any future Campaign already paid for, or any portion of a yearly Subscription already paid, except where expressly provided in this Policy, the Agreement, or required by applicable law.

**4. Cancellation by Winnbell**

**4.1 Cancellation for Cause**

The Provider may suspend or terminate a Participating Business's Subscription or participation in one or more Campaigns where permitted under the Agreement, including for breach of the Agreement, fraud, misuse, non-payment, legal or regulatory requirements, or other justified grounds.

Unless otherwise required by applicable law or expressly stated in the Agreement, the Participating Business will not be entitled to a refund of any subscription fees already paid.

**4.2 Cancellation for Convenience**

If the Provider cancels a Participating Business's Subscription for reasons not attributable to the Participating Business, the Provider will refund any prepaid subscription fees relating to Campaigns that have not yet commenced, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties.

**4.3 Determination of Non-Commenced Campaigns**

For the purposes of Section 4.2, a Campaign will be considered to have not yet commenced if its scheduled start date has not occurred as of the effective date of the Provider's cancellation.

**5. General Principles**

**5.1 Nature of Fees**

Subscription fees are paid for access to the Services and participation opportunities made available through the Platform and are not contingent upon any particular commercial outcome, level of participation, or Campaign performance.

**5.2 Effect of Cancellation**

Cancellation or termination affects future participation only unless expressly stated otherwise in this Policy or the Agreement.

Cancellation does not:

- reverse Services already provided;
- affect Campaigns already commenced;
- affect Entries already created or validated; or
- affect Campaign outcomes or prize administration.

**5.3 Exclusive Refund Rights**

Refunds are available only where expressly provided in this Policy, the Agreement, or as required by applicable law.

**6. Refund Processing**

**6.1 Processing Time**

Where a refund is applicable under this Policy, the Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to process the refund within thirty (30) business days after the refund becomes due.

**6.2 Method of Refund**

Unless otherwise determined by the Provider, refunds will be issued using the original payment method used for the applicable transaction.

**6.3 Automatic Refunds**

Where the Provider initiates a cancellation giving rise to a refund under this Policy, the Provider may process the applicable refund without requiring any action by the Participating Business.

**6.4 Third-Party Processing Delays**

The Participating Business acknowledges that the timing of receipt of refunded funds may be affected by payment processors or financial institutions. The Provider is not responsible for delays occurring after the refund has been processed.

**7. Policy Updates**

The Provider may update this Policy from time to time in accordance with the Agreement.
