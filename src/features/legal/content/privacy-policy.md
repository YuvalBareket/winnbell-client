**Winnbell Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated:** 16.4.2026

---

**1\. Introduction**

Winnbell ("Winnbell," "we," "us," or "our") operates a promotional campaign platform that allows users to participate in prize-based campaigns by submitting entries through participating businesses or through a free alternative method of entry.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and handle personal information when you use the Winnbell platform, including our website, services, and related features (the "Platform").

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "users" includes both individual participants in campaigns and representatives of participating businesses (such as account holders, employees, or other authorized contacts) who access or interact with the Platform. Unless otherwise specified, references to "users" in this Policy include both individual participants and representatives of participating businesses.

Winnbell applies a consistent, high standard of privacy protection across its Platform. This Policy is designed to reflect widely recognized privacy principles, including those established under California privacy laws, and is intended to apply to users regardless of their location.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information. Winnbell makes these rights available to users as described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal limitations and operational requirements.

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

The Platform is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and Winnbell does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

---

**2\. Personal Information**

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "Personal Information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular individual or household. It does not include: information that is lawfully made available from government records; de-identified or aggregated information that cannot reasonably be used to infer information about, or otherwise be linked to, a particular individual or household, provided that the information is maintained and used in de-identified or aggregated form and is not re-identified; or information that does not identify and is not reasonably capable of being associated with, or linked to, a particular individual or household.

**6\. How We Use Personal Information**

We use personal information to operate the Platform, administer promotional campaigns, and ensure the integrity and proper functioning of Winnbell services.

**6.1 Platform Operation and Account Management**

- *Categories Used:* Identifiers; Account and Registration Information; Internet or Network Activity Information; (for business users) Business and Payment Information.
- *Purpose:* Create and maintain accounts; provide access to the Platform; manage account settings and activity; provide customer support; create and manage accounts for participating businesses and their representatives.

**6.2 Entry Processing and Campaign Administration**

- *Categories Used:* Identifiers; Account and Registration Information; Entry and Transaction Information.
- *Purpose:* Receive, record, and manage entries; process AMOE requests; enforce entry limits and campaign rules; administer participation.

**6.3 Transaction Validation with Participating Businesses**

- *Categories Used:* Identifiers (limited); Entry and Transaction Information; Verification Information (when needed).
- *Purpose:* Validate transaction-based entries; confirm submitted information corresponds to qualifying activity; verify eligibility.

**6.4 Fraud Prevention and Behavioral Risk Assessment**

- *Categories Used:* Identifiers; Account and Registration Information; Entry and Transaction Information; Internet or Network Activity Information; Geolocation / Residency Information; Inferences and Derived Information.
- *Purpose:* Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or manipulation; secure accounts; protect the Platform; maintain fairness and integrity; identify and assess potential affiliation between users and participating businesses.

**6.5 Winner Selection, Verification, and Prize Fulfillment**

- *Categories Used:* Identifiers; Account and Registration Information; Entry and Transaction Information; Verification Information; (as applicable) Sensitive Personal Information.
- *Purpose:* Select winners; verify identity, eligibility, residency, affiliation with participating businesses, and entry validity; communicate with winners; fulfill prizes; arrange and complete prize fulfillment.

**6.6 Business Insights and Analytics**

- *Categories Used:* Internet or Network Activity Information; Entry and Transaction Information; Inferences and Derived Information (typically in aggregated or de-identified form).
- *Purpose:* Understand Platform usage; generate aggregate or de-identified insights; evaluate engagement and campaign performance; improve platform operations.

**6.7 Communications**

- *Categories Used:* Identifiers; Account and Registration Information; (for business users) Business and Payment Information.
- *Purpose:* Send service-related messages; send campaign updates; send marketing communications where permitted by law; communicate with participating businesses about account activity, billing, verification requests, and updates; send promotional communications where permitted.

**6.8 Compliance, Eligibility, and Security**

- *Categories Used:* Any categories reasonably necessary for compliance.
- *Purpose:* Enforce geographic restrictions and eligibility requirements, including age verification, minimum age restrictions, and affiliation-based eligibility restrictions; comply with legal obligations; protect the Platform and its users; process billing, payments, and related administrative functions associated with participating business accounts.

**7\. Sharing/Disclosures of Personal Information**

We disclose personal information only where necessary to operate the Platform, administer campaigns, and comply with legal obligations. We do not provide personal information for independent marketing or commercial use by participating businesses.

Winnbell acts as an independent controller of personal information processed through the Platform. Participating businesses are independent third parties and act as separate controllers with respect to any personal information they collect or process independently. Participating businesses do not receive access to personal information held by Winnbell except where strictly necessary for validation and operational purposes as described in this Policy.

**7.1 Participating Businesses (Validation Only)**

We may share limited information with participating businesses to:

- validate transaction-based entries
- confirm eligibility and transaction consistency
- confirm, where necessary, whether a user is affiliated with the participating business

In certain cases, including winner verification or elevated risk scenarios, we may request that a participating business review its internal records to confirm transaction details necessary to validate eligibility, including, where necessary, confirmation of whether a user is affiliated with the participating business for purposes of enforcing applicable campaign rules.

Winnbell does not receive full transaction records or unrelated personal information from participating businesses.

Such sharing is limited to operational purposes and does not provide businesses with independent access to user data.

Participating businesses do not receive ongoing access to user personal information and may not retain, use, or disclose any information obtained through validation for independent purposes.

Winnbell may rely on limited information provided by participating businesses for the purpose of validating entries as part of its platform operations.

**7.2 Aggregated and De-Identified Business Insights**

We may provide participating businesses with aggregated or de-identified insights related to campaign performance and engagement.

Such information is processed in a manner designed to prevent identification of individual users and does not include personal information.

**7.3 Service Providers/Contractors**

We may share information with service providers supporting infrastructure, analytics, communications, and verification. These providers act on our behalf and are not permitted to use data independently.

We may also share personal information related to participating businesses and their representatives with service providers where necessary to support account management, payment processing, billing, infrastructure, analytics, and platform operations.

Payment-related information is processed by third-party payment service providers acting on our behalf. Winnbell does not store full payment card details.

**7.4 Legal, Compliance, and Protection Disclosures**

We may disclose information to comply with legal obligations, enforce our rules, prevent fraud, or protect rights and safety.

**7.5 Business Transfers**

Personal information may be transferred in connection with mergers, acquisitions, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets.

**9\. Data Retention**

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Platform, administer campaigns, maintain system integrity, and comply with legal obligations.

**9.1 Retention Principles**

We retain personal information based on the nature of the data and the purpose for which it was collected. In general, we keep information for as long as needed to:

- provide and operate the Platform
- manage campaign participation and entries
- verify eligibility and process prize fulfillment
- maintain security and prevent misuse
- comply with legal, regulatory, and operational requirements
- manage participating business accounts, including billing, payments, and ongoing campaign participation

**9.2 Retention by Category of Personal Information**

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of personal information, including:

- **Account and identifier information:** retained for the duration of the account relationship and as needed to support account-related functions
- **Entry and transaction information:** retained for the duration of the campaign and for a period afterward to support validation, dispute resolution, and audit needs
- **Verification information:** retained as necessary to confirm eligibility, complete prize fulfillment, and demonstrate compliance
- **Usage and activity data:** retained to support platform performance, monitoring, and security
- **Business account and billing information:** retained for the duration of the business relationship and for additional periods as required to comply with financial, tax, accounting, and legal obligations

In general, retention periods range from the duration of the user or business relationship and active campaign participation, up to several years thereafter where necessary to comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention requirements.

**9.3 Fraud Prevention and Integrity Retention**

We may retain certain information for extended periods where necessary to:

- detect and prevent repeated or ongoing abuse
- maintain the integrity of promotional campaigns
- support internal review and enforcement processes

This may include information related to participation patterns, validation outcomes, and risk indicators.

**9.4 Legal and Compliance Retention**

We may retain personal information as required to:

- comply with legal and regulatory obligations
- resolve disputes or enforce our terms and rules
- respond to legal claims or investigations

**10\. Privacy Rights**

You may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

These rights apply to personal information relating to identifiable natural persons, including individual participants and representatives of participating businesses, where such information qualifies as personal information under applicable California law. These rights do not apply to information relating solely to legal entities.

Requests relating to business account data may be subject to separate contractual obligations under applicable agreements with participating businesses.

**10.1 Right to Know/Access**

You may request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties to whom we disclosed it.

**10.2 Right to Delete**

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain limitations.

We may retain certain information where necessary to:

- complete transactions or administer campaigns
- detect and prevent fraud or abuse
- comply with legal obligations
- enforce our terms and rules

Deletion of certain information may affect our ability to validate entries, confirm eligibility, or complete winner verification. Where required information is no longer available, this may result in an inability to confirm eligibility or, where applicable, disqualification from a campaign or prize.

**10.3 Right to Correct**

You may request correction of inaccurate personal information.

**10.4 Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing**

You may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Currently, Winnbell does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

**10.5 Right to Limit Sensitive Information**

You may request limitation of the use of sensitive personal information where applicable.

As described in this Policy, sensitive personal information is used only for verification, compliance, and security purposes. Limiting the use of such information may affect our ability to verify identity, eligibility, or transaction validity, which may result in an inability to confirm eligibility or disqualification from a campaign or prize.

**10.6 Right to Non-Discrimination**

We do not discriminate against users for exercising their privacy rights.

This means we do not deny access to the Platform, provide a different level of service, or treat users differently solely because they have exercised their rights under this Privacy Policy.

However, certain Platform functions require sufficient information to verify eligibility and compliance with campaign rules. If necessary, information is unavailable due to a user request, we may be unable to validate participation or confirm eligibility. Any resulting limitation, including disqualification from a campaign or prize, is based on the inability to complete required verification processes and not on the exercise of privacy rights.
