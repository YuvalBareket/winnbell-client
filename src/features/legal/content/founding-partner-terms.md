# Winnbell Founding Partner Special Terms

These Founding Partner Special Terms (the "Special Terms") apply to a Participating Business that enrolls in the Winnbell Founding Partner Plan.

These Special Terms supplement and form part of the Winnbell Participating Business Agreement, including the Winnbell Cancellation & Refund Policy incorporated into that Agreement.

Capitalized terms not defined in these Special Terms have the meanings assigned to them in the Participating Business Agreement.

## 1. Order of Precedence

These Special Terms modify the Participating Business Agreement and Cancellation & Refund Policy only with respect to the matters expressly addressed below.

If these Special Terms conflict with the Participating Business Agreement or Cancellation & Refund Policy, these Special Terms will control to the extent of that conflict.

All provisions of the Participating Business Agreement and Cancellation & Refund Policy that are not expressly modified by these Special Terms remain unchanged and fully applicable.

## 2. Founding Partner Subscription

The Founding Partner Plan is a fixed three-month Subscription offered at a special Founding Partner price.

The Participating Business is enrolling for one three-month Subscription Term and not on a month-to-month basis.

The Founding Partner Subscription does not automatically renew at the end of the initial three-month term.

Any monthly equivalent displayed in connection with the Founding Partner Plan is provided for comparison purposes only and does not convert the Founding Partner Plan into a monthly subscription.

## 3. Immediate Payment

Unlike standard Subscriptions that may be charged on the applicable Auto Payment Date, the full Subscription Fee for the Founding Partner Subscription will be charged immediately upon enrollment.

By accepting these Special Terms, the Participating Business authorizes Winnbell to process the full Founding Partner Subscription Fee immediately using the payment method provided during enrollment.

## 4. Cancellation and Refunds

After the Founding Partner Subscription Fee has been successfully processed, the standard Winnbell Cancellation & Refund Policy applies.

Because the Founding Partner Subscription is a fixed three-month Subscription with no automatic renewal, cancellation does not create a right to end the current paid Subscription Term early.

The Participating Business will not be entitled to a refund, prorated refund, credit, or adjustment for the current paid Subscription Term merely because it stops participating, discontinues use of the Services, or requests cancellation before the end of that term.

The Services will remain available through the end of the current paid Subscription Term, subject to the Participating Business Agreement.

Any refund rights, exceptions, or financial adjustments not expressly addressed in these Special Terms will be governed by the Winnbell Cancellation & Refund Policy and applicable law.

## 5. End of Initial Term

The Founding Partner Subscription will expire automatically at the end of the initial three-month Subscription Term unless the Participating Business separately accepts a renewal offer from Winnbell.

No renewal payment will be charged automatically under these Special Terms.

Continued access to the Services after the end of the initial term will require a new renewal acceptance, enrollment, payment authorization, or other action specified by Winnbell.

## 6. Potential Renewal Offer

Winnbell may offer the Participating Business the opportunity to renew its Subscription for one additional twelve-month term at the same Founding Partner monthly rate as the initial term.

Any such renewal opportunity is subject to:

- the Participating Business remaining eligible and in compliance with the Participating Business Agreement;
- the renewal offer being made available by Winnbell;
- the Participating Business accepting the renewal offer within the period specified by Winnbell;
- completion of any required enrollment, payment authorization, or payment process; and
- acceptance of any applicable renewal terms presented by Winnbell.

A renewal will not become effective unless and until the Participating Business affirmatively accepts the renewal offer and the required payment is successfully processed.

## 7. Expiration of Renewal Opportunity

Any offer to renew at the Founding Partner price may be subject to an acceptance deadline stated in the renewal offer.

If the Participating Business does not accept and complete the renewal process within the stated period, the Founding Partner renewal opportunity may expire.

If the Participating Business later seeks to re-enroll after the renewal opportunity has expired, Winnbell will have no obligation to restore the Founding Partner price. Any later enrollment will be subject to the pricing, plans, availability, and terms offered by Winnbell at that time.

## 8. No Obligation to Continue Beyond the Initial Term

The Participating Business is not obligated to purchase a second Subscription Term.

Likewise, except for any renewal opportunity expressly offered under Section 6, Winnbell is not obligated to continue providing the Services after the end of the initial three-month Subscription Term.

## 9. Electronic Acceptance

By checking the box indicating acceptance of these Special Terms, clicking an acceptance or enrollment button, or otherwise electronically manifesting assent, the individual completing enrollment:

- agrees to these Special Terms on behalf of the Participating Business;
- represents that they are authorized to bind the Participating Business;
- acknowledges that the Founding Partner Plan is a fixed three-month Subscription;
- authorizes immediate collection of the full Subscription Fee;
- acknowledges that the Subscription does not automatically renew; and
- acknowledges that voluntary discontinuation of use does not create a right to a refund or prorated refund for the current paid Subscription Term.

Electronic acceptance of these Special Terms will have the same legal force and effect as a handwritten signature.
