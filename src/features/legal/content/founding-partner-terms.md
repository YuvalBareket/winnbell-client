**WINNBELL FOUNDING PARTNER SPECIAL TERMS**

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

These Founding Partner Special Terms (the "Special Terms") supplement and form part of the Winnbell [Participating Business Agreement](/business-agreement) and the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/cancellation) incorporated into that Agreement.

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings assigned to them in the Participating Business Agreement.

**1\. Priority**

These Special Terms control if they conflict with the Participating Business Agreement or Cancellation & Refund Policy regarding the Founding Partner Plan. All other provisions remain unchanged.

**2\. Founding Partner Plan**

The Founding Partner Plan applies separately to each participating business location or branch enrolled in Winnbell.

For each enrolled location, the Founding Partner Plan consists of:

- a fixed three-month Subscription Term;
- a price of $100 per month;
- a total upfront Subscription Fee of $300; and
- an Entry Cap of 2,500 Entries per Campaign.

The Subscription Fee and Entry Cap are calculated separately for each enrolled location.

The Plan is not month-to-month and does not automatically renew.

**3\. Immediate Payment**

The full $300 Subscription Fee will be charged immediately upon enrollment using the payment method provided by the Participating Business.

**4\. Cancellation**

Cancellation does not end the initial three-month Subscription Term early and does not create a right to a refund, prorated refund, credit, or adjustment.

The Subscription will remain available until the end of the paid three-month term, subject to the Participating Business Agreement.

**5\. Potential Renewal Offer**

Winnbell may offer the Participating Business the opportunity to continue for one additional twelve-month term at the same price of $100 per month.

Any renewal offer:

- is not guaranteed;
- must be affirmatively accepted by the Participating Business;
- may require a new payment authorization or payment process;
- may be subject to an acceptance deadline; and
- will not renew automatically.

The renewal will become effective only after the Participating Business accepts the offer and the required payment is successfully processed.

**6\. Loss of Founding Partner Renewal Eligibility**

If the Participating Business submits a cancellation, non-renewal, or discontinuation request at any time, it will permanently lose eligibility for any future renewal at the Founding Partner price.

Withdrawing or reversing the request will not require Winnbell to restore that opportunity.

Any later enrollment will be subject to the pricing, plans, Entry Caps, and terms available at that time.

**7\. Electronic Acceptance**

By electronically accepting these Special Terms, the individual completing enrollment:

- agrees to these Special Terms on behalf of the Participating Business;
- represents that they are authorized to bind the Participating Business;
- authorizes the immediate $300 charge; and
- acknowledges that the Plan is a fixed three-month Subscription that does not automatically renew.
