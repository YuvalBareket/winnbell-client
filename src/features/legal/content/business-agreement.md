**1\. PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

**1.1 Purpose.** The Provider operates a centralized promotional campaign platform (the "**Platform**") through which participating businesses may enable eligible customers to access participation opportunities in promotional prize campaigns (each, a "**Campaign**") operated and administered solely by the Provider.

**1.2 Scope.** This Agreement governs the Participating Business's access to and use of the Platform, services, tools, Campaign participation features, and related offerings made available to the Participating Business in connection with the Campaigns (collectively, the "**Services**").

**1.3 Future Campaigns.** This Agreement applies to the Participating Business's participation in all current and future campaigns made available by the Provider and elected by the Participating Business during the Term, unless otherwise expressly stated in a signed order form, campaign enrollment confirmation, or custom addendum executed by both Parties.

**1.4 Nature of Relationship.** The Participating Business is purchasing access to the Services and participation opportunities within the Campaigns. It is not purchasing entries, chances to win, or any guaranteed commercial outcome.

**10\. ENTRY CAPS AND SERVICE FULFILLMENT**

**10.1 Entry Cap Allocation.** The Provider may allocate to the Participating Business a maximum number of entry opportunities, submissions, recognitions, or countable Entries for a campaign (an "**Entry Cap**"), as specified in the applicable order form, configuration, or platform setting.

**10.2 Nature of Entry Cap.** An Entry Cap is a participation capacity limit only. It is not a guarantee of any number of actual Entries, participants, transactions or customer actions.

**10.3 Application of Entry Cap.** The Provider will determine how Entry Caps are applied, counted, reduced, exhausted, or otherwise administered in accordance with the campaign configuration, Official Rules, and the Provider's validation logic.

**10.4 Exhaustion.** Once the Participating Business's Entry Cap is exhausted for a campaign:

(a) no additional Entries associated with the Participating Business will be recognized for that campaign;

(b) the Participating Business may be removed from active campaign visibility or otherwise limited operationally; and

(c) the Provider's campaign-related Services for that campaign will be deemed substantially fulfilled, subject to any remaining post-campaign obligations under this Agreement.

**10.5 No Carryover.** Unused Entry Cap capacity has no cash value, does not roll over, and does not carry forward unless expressly stated in a signed writing by the Provider.

**10.6 No Refund for Utilization Rate.** Except as expressly provided in the Cancellation Policy or a signed writing, the Participating Business is not entitled to any refund, credit or adjustment based on the rate of utilization, timing of exhaustion, or number of Entries ultimately created.

**Winnbell - Business Participation Guidelines**

**1\. Purpose**

These Guidelines provide practical instructions for Participating Businesses on how to promote and operate within Winnbell Campaigns. They form part of the Participating Business Agreement and must be followed as a condition of participation. Capitalized terms in this agreement shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the Agreement.

Winnbell operates all Campaigns as the sole operator and administrator. Participating Businesses act only as marketing partners that enable customer access to participation opportunities.

**2\. Core Principles**

Participating Businesses must follow these core principles:

2.1 Winnbell is fully responsible for Campaign operation, including entries and winner selection

2.2 Entries are created only through the Winnbell platform

2.3 No purchase is required to participate

2.4 Customers must not be misled about how participation works

**3\. Staff Behavior and Day-to-Day Conduct**

3.1 Participating Businesses must ensure that all staff:

(a) Explain the Campaign in a simple and accurate way.

(b) Do not improvise or create their own explanations of how the Campaign works.

(c) Do not make promises about winning or chances of winning.

(d) Do not suggest that a purchase is required to participate.

(e) Treat the Campaign as a Winnbell promotion, not a promotion run by the business.

(f) If unsure, staff should not improvise or create their own explanations and should direct customers to the Winnbell platform.

3.2 Transactions must be real, normal customer transactions. Staff must not create or assist with fake or unusual transactions to help generate participation.

**4\. Prohibited Conduct**

4.1 Participating Businesses must not:

(a) present any Campaign as their own promotion;

(b) state or imply that customers must buy something to participate;

(c) suggest that buying more increases the chance of winning beyond the Campaign rules;

(d) create, issue or attempt to control entries;

(e) help customers submit false or misleading information;

(f) misrepresent how a Campaign works in any way;

(g) condition, restrict or present any Winnbell additional participation mechanism (including QR codes or links) as dependent on a purchase, transaction; or customer action; or

(h) represent additional participation mechanisms, included Bonus Entries (as defined in the official rules) as being issued, controlled or provided by the business

**5\. Customer Communication Guidelines**

5.1 All customer-facing communication must be clear, accurate and consistent with Campaign rules. Each Campaign must always be presented as part of Winnbell, not as a promotion run by the business. Marketing must not create the impression that making a purchase is required to participate.

Example:

- "Buy this to get a chance to win" — Not permitted
- "Customers can access the Winnbell Campaign through their transaction" — Correct

5.2 Businesses must not hide or ignore the fact that participation is available without purchase.

**6\. Marketing Materials and POS Promotion**

6.1 Winnbell provides pre-approved marketing materials (including posters and POS content) through the platform. Use of these materials is **strongly recommended**, as they are designed to ensure compliant and consistent communication. Businesses may use their own marketing materials, but they must fully comply with these Guidelines.

6.2 When using custom materials, businesses must include:

(a) A clear reference to Winnbell, for example:

- Winnbell logo (provided on the platform), or
- A short statement such as: "This campaign is run by Winnbell;"

(b) A clear indication that participation does not require a purchase, for example:

- "No purchase necessary. Alternative method of entry available on the Winnbell platform."

These elements ensure customers understand that the Campaign is operated by Winnbell and that free participation is available.

**7\. Cooperation**

Participating Businesses may be asked to assist with basic verification (for example, confirming a transaction). Businesses are expected to cooperate reasonably when requested.

**8\. Enforcement**

If a Participating Business does not follow these Guidelines, Winnbell may require changes to marketing or staff behavior, suspend or limit participation, or remove the business from a Campaign.

**Winnbell Cancellation & Refund Policy**

*(Participating Businesses only)*

**1\. Overview**

This Cancellation & Refund Policy (the "**Policy**") explains how cancellation of Campaign participation and Subscriptions is handled, including any applicable refunds or financial adjustments.

This Policy forms part of the Winnbell Participating Business Agreement (the "**Agreement**") and applies to all Participating Businesses. Capitalized terms in this agreement shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the Agreement.

**2\. Types of Participation**

Participating Businesses may access Winnbell Campaigns either:

- on a Campaign-by-Campaign basis, or
- through a Subscription, which provides discounted participation across multiple Campaigns

Cancellation treatment depends on the participation model and timing.

**3\. Campaign-Based Cancellation**

A Participating Business may cancel its participation in a Campaign at any time. Refund eligibility depends on timing:

- *Before Campaign Onboarding Cutoff*. If cancellation occurs before the Campaign Onboarding Cutoff, the service fee will be fully refunded.
- *After Campaign Onboarding Cutoff and Before Campaign Start*. If cancellation occurs after the Campaign Onboarding Cutoff but before the Campaign start date, the Participating Business will receive a forty percent (40%) refund of the service fee.
- *After Campaign Commencement*. Once a Campaign has commenced, no refund is available.

For the purposes of this Policy the "**Campaign Onboarding Cutoff**" is seven (7) days prior to Campaign start.

**4\. Subscription Cancellation and Adjustment**

**4.1 Subscription Principle**

Subscriptions are offered at a discounted rate, based on a commitment to participate in multiple Campaigns. If a Subscription ends before completion, the Participating Business will no longer be entitled to the subscription discount for Campaigns already consumed.

**4.2 Retainer and Adjustment Mechanism**

Subscriptions may include a retained amount (the "**Retainer**"), the Retainer means a prepaid amount collected by Winnbell at the start of a Subscription, representing payment for an additional Campaign corresponding to the final Campaign within the Subscription period. The Retainer is held by Winnbell and may be applied toward Subscription Adjustment amounts in the event of early cancellation or termination of the Subscription, in accordance with this Policy.

If a Subscription is cancelled or terminated before completion:

- Winnbell will calculate the discount already received by the Participating Business for Campaigns that have been consumed;
- This amount (the "**Subscription Adjustment**") will be deducted from the Retainer;
- Any remaining Retainer balance, if applicable, will be refunded.

For clarity:

- The Subscription Adjustment reflects the difference between:
  - the standard campaign participation fee applicable to the Participating Business at time of onboarding, and
  - the discounted subscription rate actually paid, for each consumed Campaign.
- Refunds are limited to amounts actually paid by the Participating Business.

For clarity, the Subscription Adjustment is a financial reconciliation of pricing and does not affect the validity of participation in any Campaign.

**4.3 Determination of Consumed Campaigns**

For the purpose of the Subscription Adjustment: A Campaign is considered consumed if it has passed the Campaign Onboarding Cutoff. Campaigns that have not reached the Campaign Onboarding Cutoff at the time the Subscription ends are considered unused.

**4.4 Payment Failure and Subscription Termination**

For the purposes of this Policy, a "payment failure" occurs where Winnbell is unable to successfully process or receive the applicable payment when due, including due to insufficient funds, declined or expired payment methods, bank or payment processor rejection, failure to complete any required authentication or authorization step, or failure by the Participating Business to take any action necessary to complete the payment.

(a) If a Subscription Payment failure occurs:

- Winnbell may provide an opportunity to complete payment prior to the Campaign start date;
- If payment is not completed, participation in that Campaign may be suspended, and no service fee will be charged for that Campaign.

(b) If a Payment failure occurs again for a subsequent Campaign, the Subscription will be treated as terminated.

(c) For the purpose of the Subscription Adjustment:

- any Campaign suspended due to non-payment will be treated as consumed;
- all subsequent Campaigns will be treated as unused.

**5\. Cancellation by Winnbell**

**5.1 Cancellation for Cause**

Winnbell may suspend or terminate participation due to:

- breach of the Agreement;
- non-compliance with applicable rules or guidelines; or
- fraud, misuse, or prohibited conduct.

In such cases, refunds (if any) will be determined in accordance with this Policy and the Agreement, taking into account services already provided.

**5.2 Cancellation for Convenience**

If Winnbell cancels participation for reasons not related to the Participating Business:

- any service fees for affected Campaigns will be fully refunded and
- any remaining Retainer will be fully refunded.

**6\. Effect of Cancellation**

Cancellation applies to **future participation only**. For clarity:

- Campaigns that have already commenced or passed the onboarding cutoff are not affected.
- Services already provided are not reversed.
- Campaign operation, entries, and outcomes remain unaffected.

7.1 Fees are paid for access to Winnbell's Services and participation opportunities and are not tied to outcomes or performance.

7.2 Subscription discounts are conditional upon completion of the applicable Subscription period.
